InfinityPaper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version 1.5

Your drawings stay with you

InfinityPaper does not collect or transmit your drawings, reference images, or personal data to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Local storage

Drawings, reference images, and previews are stored in the app’s local storage. Brush choices and other preferences are saved on your device. Device backups may include this data according to your Apple backup settings.

Photos, files & sharing

The app imports only the images you choose through Apple’s Photos or Files picker. Imported images are resized and saved with the drawing. When you export or share, the file is sent to the app, person, or location you choose. Those services handle it under their own policies.

Deleting data

Delete a drawing from My drawings to remove its locally stored content. Removing the app removes its local app data. Copies you exported, shared, or backed up are managed separately.

Policy updates

This policy describes this version of InfinityPaper. If the app’s data practices change, its privacy policy and App Store disclosures will be updated.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact sgazza@outlook.com.

This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting this page is separate from using the app; the hosting provider may process standard web request information under its own privacy statement. This page does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.
